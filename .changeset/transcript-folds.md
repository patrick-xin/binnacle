A person folds the transcript's events, and moves through them by key or click. While the transcript has the Focus, its newest event is Marked in inverse video. Up and down move the Mark, and the transcript scrolls to keep the Marked event in view. Enter folds the Marked event to its one header line, `▸ #<seq> <type> (<n> lines)`, where `<n>` counts the JSON lines the Fold hides. A click on an event's header line folds or unfolds that event, and Marks it.
