The Kit exports `frame` and `rule`. `frame(binnacle, lines, width, { title, tone })` draws lines in a box of the theme's edge, with a title in its top edge, and the edge in the `border` Tone or in `tone`. `rule(binnacle, text, width)` draws a Title's rule, which the Title's default Look now draws through. The type `FrameOptions` names the options.
