'binnacle': minor

An ask fits the room it is given, however much it holds: its edges are always drawn, its prose is paged by `shift+↑`/`shift+↓` keeping a row of the last page, and its offers show in a window that follows focus, saying where it is on the bottom edge — PageUp and PageDown jumping a window's worth. The composer's place gives an ask `max(12, rows − 10)` rows and the dialog four fifths of the terminal's. A view's ask may name its own `rows` (from three), a theme may give every ask one with `asks.rows`, and the theme gains the words `page`, `offer.at` and `page.at`.
